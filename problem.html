Research question

Given a date and a rail line, will that day be Low, Medium or High crowd compared with the line's normal?

Scope: the data counts passengers for a whole line per day. It says nothing about single stations or hours of the day, so "crowd level" here means daily volume on that line.

The label: crowd level

Defined per line, from training days only

Rule

Low  daily passengers ≤ the line's 33rd percentile

Medium  in between

High  daily passengers > the line's 66th percentile

Why per line, and why percentiles

A busy day for BTS Gold Line (about 7 thousand passengers) is a quiet day for BTS Green Line (about 650 thousand). Percentiles make levels comparable across lines and are easy to explain. They are calculated on training days only, so the test period cannot influence them.
